How to Get the Gate-trigger Signal?

  2. A saw-tooth generator produces vstv_{st}vst​, which starts from zero at every zero crossing of the supply.
  3. A comparator compares vstv_{st}vst​ with the control voltage vcontrolv_{control}vcontrol​ and gives a gate pulse at the crossing point.
